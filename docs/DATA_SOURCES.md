# World Number - Authoritative Data Sources & Licensing Register
**Document Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Primary International Providers

| Domain | Primary Source Organization | Datasets Used | Licensing / Access Terms | Update Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Population** | United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs (UN DESA) | World Population Prospects (WPP 2024 Revision) | Open Access (CC BY 3.0 IGO) | Biennial / Annual Estimates |
| **Economy** | World Bank & International Monetary Fund (IMF) | World Development Indicators (WDI), World Economic Outlook (WEO 2024) | CC BY 4.0 International / IMF Open Data | Bi-annual (April/October) |
| **Environment** | Global Carbon Project, FAO, UNEP | Global Carbon Budget, Global Forest Resources Assessment | Open Access scientific data / CC BY-NC-SA | Annual |
| **Health** | World Health Organization (WHO) | Global Health Observatory (GHO) | Open Access (WHO Open Data) | Ongoing / Annual |
| **Education** | UNESCO Institute for Statistics (UIS) | UIS Global Education Database | CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO | Annual |
| **Technology** | International Telecommunication Union (ITU) | World Telecommunication/ICT Indicators | Open Data Initiative | Annual |
| **Military** | Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI) | SIPRI Military Expenditure Database | Non-commercial open access with attribution | Annual (April) |
| **Global Indices** | UNDP, Transparency International, RSF | Human Development Reports (HDR 2024), CPI, Press Freedom Index | Open Access with attribution | Annual |
| **Geography** | UN Statistics Division, Natural Earth | Standard Country Area & Demarcation | Public Domain | Stable |
| **Tourism** | UN Tourism (UNWTO) | World Tourism Barometer | UN Open Data | Monthly / Annual |
| **Energy** | Energy Institute / Ember / IEA | Statistical Review of World Energy, Global Electricity Review | CC BY 4.0 International | Annual |
| **Agriculture** | UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) | FAOSTAT Database | CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO | Annual |
| **Politics** | Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU), Freedom House | Parline Database, Freedom in the World | Open Access with attribution | Monthly / Annual |
| **Infrastructure** | World Bank | Logistics Performance Index (LPI), Transport Data | Open Data Commons | Biennial |
| **Climate** | Copernicus Climate Change Service (ERA5), WMO | State of the Global Climate, ERA5 Reanalysis | Open Access (Copernicus License) | Monthly / Annual |
| **Employment** | International Labour Organization (ILO) | ILOSTAT Database | CC BY 4.0 International | Quarterly / Annual |
| **Science** | WIPO, UNESCO, Scimago | Global Innovation Index, Science Report | Open Access with attribution | Annual |
| **Sports** | International Olympic Committee (IOC), FIFA | Historical Olympic Database, World Rankings | Official Federation Public Releases | Periodic / Continuous |
| **City Rankings** | UN-Habitat, Demographia | World Cities Report, Urban Areas Agglomerations | Open Access with attribution | Biennial |
| **UN SDG Goals** | UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN) | Sustainable Development Report (Cambridge Univ. Press) | CC BY-NC 4.0 | Annual |
